# Decisions

This is the decision table of the project's research record, published so that every decision cited in the specifications, study reports and READMEs (D-01 to D-63) can be looked up. Each row gives the decision, its date, its status, the reason and its consequences. Each was recorded before the result it governs was produced.

The rest of the research record is not published. Where a row cites a section of it (for example "§8" or "§9.3"), that section is not available here; the published evidence for each study is in its folder under `results/experiments/` and in the specifications next to each model.

**The rows are copied byte for byte from the record and must not be edited.** The RNN study pins the SHA-256 of each row it depends on, in `results/experiments/rnn_study/study_conditions.json` and `test_evaluation/freeze.json`, so any reader can check that the published rows are the ones that authorised the study. The section headings below (`## 11.` and the closing `## 12.`) are kept because the study's checker locates the table by them.
